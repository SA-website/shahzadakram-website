## What this covers

Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) — making sure content is structured, sourced, and written in a way that AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, and similar) can accurately extract, cite, and attribute back to the source. This overlaps heavily with technical SEO and content clarity, but has its own emerging best practices around structured data, direct-answer formatting, and source credibility signals.
